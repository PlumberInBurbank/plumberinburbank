Leak repair in Burbank

A leak rarely announces itself politely. It shows up as a stain spreading on a ceiling, a damp patch that never dries, a water bill that climbs for no reason, or a meter dial that creeps while every tap is off. Our leak repair service in Burbank starts with finding the true source — because repairing the wrong spot just buys the leak more time.


Signs you may have a hidden leak

  • Water bill rising with no change in use
  • Damp, warm or discolored patches on floors, walls or ceilings
  • The sound of water when everything is off
  • Musty smells in one room that cleaning never fixes
  • A water meter that moves with all taps closed (the classic meter test)

How we handle it

Detection first — pressure tests and targeted checks before anything is opened up. Then a clear explanation of what failed, one upfront price, and a repair that addresses the cause, not just the wet patch. In older Burbank homes the cause is often corroded or shifted pipe; in slab homes it can be a line under the floor that needs rerouting rather than repeated patching.
