Boiler care in hard-water country

A boiler's closed loop protects it from constant fresh mineral load — but only while the loop stays tight. Leaks pull in makeup water and fresh scale with it, and skipped maintenance lets corrosion inhibitor fade and sludge build up. Annual service — burner and heat exchanger checked, expansion tank pressure set, inhibitor topped up, magnetic filter cleaned — is what keeps a Burbank boiler quiet and efficient.


Call us if you notice

  • Pressure dropping and needing frequent top-ups
  • Short-cycling — firing, stopping, firing again
  • Cold radiators or uneven heat room to room
  • Banging, whistling or kettling sounds
