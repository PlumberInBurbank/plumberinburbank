Water filter cleaning in Burbank

A whole-house filter only protects your plumbing while it's maintained. Our water filter cleaning service in Burbank follows the schedule each system type actually needs — housings sanitized at every visit, washable media cleaned, spent cartridges replaced with the correct type.


The Burbank schedule

  • Sediment cartridges: every 3 to 6 months
  • Carbon cartridges: every 6 to 12 months — replacement only, carbon can't be washed back to life
  • RO prefilters: every 6 to 12 months; membrane: every 2 to 3 years
  • Water softeners: deep clean about once a year, salt checked regularly
